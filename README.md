## Problem Statement
Reduce the time to confirm truthfully information.

## Operational Interpretation
IA Detector reduces the time required to verify suspicious digital content by helping journalists analyze claims, compare evidence, detect risk signals, and classify the case as `PASS`, `NO_PASS`, or `HUMAN_REVIEW`.

## Product Definition
IA Detector is an AI-assisted verification platform for journalists and editorial teams. It helps analyze suspicious digital content, search evidence, identify risk signals and classify cases as PASS, NO_PASS or HUMAN_REVIEW.
