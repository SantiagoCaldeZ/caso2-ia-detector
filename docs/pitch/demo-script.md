## Problem Story

A journalist receives a suspicious claim through a digital channel shortly before publication. The information looks urgent, but the source is unclear and there is not enough time to manually search multiple fact-checking websites, compare evidence, and document the decision.

IA Detector helps reduce that verification time by centralizing the claim, evidence, risk signals, scores, and final operational classification in one workflow.

---

## MVP Demo Flow

1. Log in as `journalist@iadetector.local`.
2. Submit suspicious text and show a `PASS` result.
3. Submit a suspicious URL and show a `NO_PASS` result.
4. Upload an image or screenshot and show a `HUMAN_REVIEW` result.
5. Open verification history.
6. Open a previous case and show evidence, risk signals, scores, and audit trace.

---

## Key Message

IA Detector is not a truth machine. It does not return `TRUE` or `FALSE`. It helps editorial teams decide faster and more responsibly by classifying cases as `PASS`, `NO_PASS`, or `HUMAN_REVIEW`, always showing the evidence behind the result.

---

## Benefits

- Reduces time spent searching for evidence.
- Organizes fact-checking information in one place.
- Makes uncertainty visible through risk signals.
- Supports editorial responsibility instead of replacing it.
- Works locally for MVP using mocks and deterministic demo data.

---

## Call to Action

Use IA Detector as an editorial verification assistant for teams that need to react quickly without sacrificing evidence quality, traceability, or human judgment.
